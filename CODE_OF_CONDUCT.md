# Code of conduct

3den is built for people working together, and building it should feel
the same way. This applies to everyone in the project's spaces: issues,
pull requests, chats, and events.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good intent. Criticize ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome newcomers, and remember everyone started somewhere.
- Listen, especially to people whose experience differs from yours.
- When you get something wrong, own it and fix it.

## What isn't okay

- Harassment, threats, or insults, in public or in private messages.
- Discrimination or slurs based on who someone is: race, ethnicity,
  religion, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, age,
  appearance, nationality, or anything else.
- Sexual language or attention that isn't welcome.
- Publishing someone's private information (where they live, their real
  name if they don't use it here) without their permission.
- Repeatedly derailing discussions, or pushing on after being asked to stop.

## If something happens

Write to **conduct@3den.example** (TO DO: replace with the real address
before the repository is public). Reports are kept private. The
maintainers will look into every report and may warn, suspend, or remove
anyone who breaks these rules, from any project space, for as long as
they decide.

Maintainers who don't follow this code can be removed from the project.
