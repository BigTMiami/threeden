On your own iPhone

  1. Open 3den in Safari on the iPhone.
  2. Tap Share (the square with an arrow), then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add. 3den now opens from its icon, full screen, like an app.

It's still the prototype: everything is saved in that phone's browser only.
